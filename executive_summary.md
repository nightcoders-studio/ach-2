# Executive Summary – KeudeKu

**Platform:** KeudeKu – F&B Management Platform for Indonesian UMKM

**Vision:** Enable UMKM F&B businesses to operate digitally with the efficiency, customer intelligence, and operational visibility of large restaurant chains.

**Mission:**
- Digitally manage operations (menu, POS, staff, payments)
- Boost customer retention through personalized AI recommendations & loyalty
- Provide real‑time analytics and actionable business insights
- Streamline payments and reduce operational inefficiencies

**Target Users:**
- Business owners (café, coffee shop, restaurant, food‑stall)
- Cashiers & staff
- End‑customers who order via QR‑menu

**Key Value Propositions:**
- Smart QR‑Menu that becomes a data‑driven sales engine
- Integrated POS & payment processing (cash, QRIS, e‑wallet)
- AI‑powered recommendation engine
- Loyalty & feedback management
- Comprehensive analytics dashboard

**Cultural & Brand Identity:** Modern professional SaaS with subtle Acehnese visual cues (Rumoh Aceh roof silhouette, Pinto‑style geometry). Dark‑mode palette: Deep Espresso #1A1615, Warm Latte #FDF8F5, Mocha Brown #4A3E3D, Soft Amber #C9822B.

**Technology Stack:**
- Frontend – Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Shadcn UI
- Backend – Supabase (Auth + Postgres + Serverless functions) / optional NestJS for future expansion
- Deployment – Vercel (frontend) + Supabase hosting, Docker containerization for CI/CD via GitHub Actions

**Deliverables:** Executive Summary, PRD, BRD, Functional/Non‑Functional Specs, User Stories, Architecture diagrams, Database schema, UML diagrams, API spec, Design System, User Flows, Roadmap, QA, Security, Monitoring & Deployment plans.
